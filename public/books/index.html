The books

Step by step training guides by Mat Harvey. Each one breaks a skill into six levels.

Book 1

The Handstand

Six levels to a 60 second freestanding handstand.

This book breaks the handstand into six levels, from your first protracted plank to holding a freestanding handstand for a full minute. Each level gives you a clear workout, photos of the exercises, links to demonstration videos and a graduation test, so you always know where you are and what to do next.

This takes time. It can take the average adult 6 months to 2 years of regular practice, 4 to 7 days a week, at least 30 minutes a day.

Book 2

The Ring Muscle Up

Six levels to a strict ring muscle up, with no kipping.

This book breaks the ring muscle up into six levels, from your first ring hang to strict, controlled muscle ups and a conditioning workout to help make them feel easier. Each level gives you a clear workout, photos of the exercises, links to demonstration videos and a graduation test.

It is built around strict strength and patience, with care for your elbows and wrists. For most people starting from scratch it takes many months.

How each book works

Each book starts with a benchmark test that tells you which level to begin at. Every level has a workout, photos of the exercises, links to demonstration videos and a graduation test. Pass the test and move up a level.
