The Story

Behind The Gym

I started my adult life as a scientist. My 30's brought a change where I decided to pursue coaching and sport full-time.

"A few years ago I broke my back. Overnight I went from a ring routine and handstand to not even one pushup. Now I'm back and stronger than ever."

I started The Bodyweight Gym to pursue an ideal: that everyone can develop themselves physically with simple equipment, the right education, and support. When COVID closed our physical gym, we transformed it into this digital space.
