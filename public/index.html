The story

Behind the gym

A few years ago I broke my back. Overnight I went from a ring routine and handstand to not even one pushup. Now I’m back and stronger than ever. Mat Harvey, founder of The Bodyweight Gym

I started my adult life as a scientist. My 30s brought a change where I decided to pursue coaching and sport full-time.

I started The Bodyweight Gym to pursue an ideal: that everyone can develop themselves physically with simple equipment, the right education, and support. When COVID closed our physical gym, we turned it into this digital space.

The classes stay free. The books are for when you want a step by step plan for one skill.
